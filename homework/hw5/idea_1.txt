Idea 1

Market and scope
    Mexican REIT's also known as Fibras Inmobiliarias Mexicanas
    Mexican Peso
    Sector can vary as Fibras can be found in different options such as industry,
    logistics, tourism, education jus to mention a few.

Investment thesis
    The expectation is to identify sustained growth and revenue which can
    translate into healthy indicators to acquire/sell this type of stock. Aligning
    your choice with your investment objectives.

Potential edge
    Invest semi-passively in a long term horizon without day trading, make adjustments
    quarterly for starters, adjust according to your needs once familiar with the process.

    Data is publicly available through quarterly and annual reports so you can
    automate this process with custom options.

Research approach
    This idea does not consider a model, just a simple analysis of indicators
    to make you more familiar with this type of investment.
    
    Where the value is located is in identifying on your own what indicators
    align with your investment objectives. In this fashion the indicators of
    your choice can then be used to train any model you would like.

Validation and risks
    The benchmark is simple the CETES rate in Mexico of your desired time period,
    if less equal to this value your selection is underperforming and you have 
    to reconsider your approach.
