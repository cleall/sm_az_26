Idea 3

Market and scope
    Mexican REIT's also known as Fibras Inmobiliarias Mexicanas
    Mexican Peso
    Sector can vary as Fibras can be found in different options such as industry,
    logistics, tourism, education jus to mention a few.

Investment thesis
    The expectation is to identify sustained growth and revenue which can
    translate into healthy indicators to acquire/sell this type of stock. Aligning
    your choice with your investment objectives. But this time identify if fusion
    of two FIBRAS in a short window does have a positive impact on a period of
    time.

Potential edge
    Invest semi-passively in a long term horizon without day trading, make adjustments
    quarterly for starters, adjust according to your needs once familiar with the process.

    Data is publicly available through quarterly and annual reports so you can
    automate this process with custom options.
    
    The signal would be to identify wether or not the price goes below/above a
    treshold, due to the fusion/acquisition of two FIBRAS. During the announcement
    to the conclusion of this process.

Research approach
    
    Where the value is located is in identifying if the fusion of two FIBRAS
    leads to an event of appreciation in a short time window. The fusion is not
    an event that happens overnight there are announcements, planned dates for
    acquisition and then final announcements. This can take months even a year.
    The result of such fusion can be measured in the short term similar to what
    IPO stock go trough when announced.

Validation and risks
    The benchmark is simple the before and after rate (of a time period) of the
    FIBRA who is acquiring the other one. In this case an optimistic fusion can
    affect several indicators and thus modify positively the rate increasing it.
    
    Compare against historic data before and after the fusion.
